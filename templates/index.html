ABOUT US

The Government Polytechnic, Solapur Library plays a vital role in supporting students and staff with access to a wide range of learning and research materials.

  • 📚 Collection Size and Scope: The library houses an extensive collection of technical, literary, and general books, along with magazines, journals, and newspapers to support all academic programs.
  • 🧾 Classification Scheme: Materials are organized using the Dewey Decimal Classification (DDC) system for efficient access and retrieval.
  • 🌐 Digital Resources: Students have access to the National Digital Library (NDL), Spoken Tutorial series, and NPTEL Online Videos to support e-learning and self-study.
  • 👨‍🏫 Library Staff: Our knowledgeable staff, led by the librarian, ensures the library remains organized, resourceful, and accessible. Contact information is available at the help desk.
  • 📖 Services Offered: Book lending, research assistance, journal access, quiet study zones, and help with referencing formats are among the many services offered.
  • 🕘 Opening Hours: The library is open from 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM on all working days.
  • 🎯 Vision & Mission: To empower students through knowledge by providing a dynamic learning space and seamless access to global information resources.

We welcome you to explore, learn, and grow with GP Library.
